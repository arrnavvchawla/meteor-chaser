    How it works

    The count

    For every 10 minutes of the night: the shower's ZHR at that solar longitude, times the sine of the radiant's altitude, times r(LM − 6.5), times the share of sky not blocked by cloud. Sporadic meteors are added on a dawn-weighted curve. Integrated over your watch.

    The darkness

    Sky glow from all 33,961 cities over 15,000 people, by Walker's law, summed within 400 km and fitted to measured sky brightness at five Southern California sites (0.09 mag rms). Converted to SQM, Bortle class and naked-eye limiting magnitude, then cut by twilight and moonlight and raised slightly by altitude.

    The weather, roads and frontier

    Live version: Open-Meteo hourly low, mid and high cloud for every site (thin high cloud counts half), temperature and wind chill; elevation; OSRM drive times, which also snap every site to a real road and drop the ones in the sea or wilderness. Sites on the Pareto frontier can't be beaten on meteors and driving at once. The pick maximises meteors minus your hourly value of driving.

    What it can't know

    Whether a gate is locked, a lot is lit, or a road is icy. Outbursts not in the catalog. Local light from a single factory. Treat counts as ±40% and check the sky before you leave.
